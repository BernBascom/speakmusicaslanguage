Will You Help Us Celebrate Her?

Bernadette Bascom, Emmy Award winning vocalist and founder of SPEAK! Music As Language, has spent nearly two decades helping children with disabilities find their voices through music. On October 11, Bothell honors her as a Hometown Hero, and we're inviting local businesses to be part of the celebration.
