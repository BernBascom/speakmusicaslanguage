A life in six chapters

Bernadette Bascom: born into the movement, raised on music, called to teach.

From a Baltimore parsonage at the heart of the civil rights struggle, to Stevie Wonder’s label, to the Las Vegas Strip, to a classroom in Bothell where children found their voices, this is the road so far.

Roots

Before Bernadette could sing a note, the two forces of her life were already in place: a father in the pulpit of the movement, and a mother at the piano.
