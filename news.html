Bothell proclaims Bernadette Bascom Day

At Bothell City Council, the proclamation was read naming Bernadette a Hometown Hero and declaring October 11 Bernadette Bascom Day. Sixty-three years after her father was arrested at Gwynn Oak for insisting every person deserves dignity, a city set aside a day for his daughter.

Spotlight · October 2026

The Wranglers

Our first spotlight belongs to the group that started it all. The Wranglers are the Northshore community singing program whose work with Bernadette was captured in the Emmy-winning KING 5 documentary Bernadette’s Touch: young people who practiced breath, rhythm, and melody until the words came, then stood on a real stage and used them.

Every student who walks into a SPEAK! class today follows the path the Wranglers cleared. This month, and every month after it, this space belongs to the students doing that work right now.
