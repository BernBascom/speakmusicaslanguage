Speak Music as Language

Transforming Early Speech Development through Innovation, Music, and Technology

"Bernadette Bascom invented an 'Intermediate Language.' She recognized the essential role these languages play in bridging the gap between different forms of communication. Her 'Intermediate Language' is music, an incredibly powerful medium that unites individuals who express themselves in different ways. Through her work, she inspired confidence, brought out the best in others, and fostered a deep connection, allowing us to communicate and understand one another on a profound level." Neel Sunderesan, Corporate Vice President of AI, Microsoft Corporation

Speak Music as Language is a groundbreaking early intervention program designed to support children with disabilities in developing speech and communication skills through an engaging, multisensory approach. Developed by acclaimed vocal coach Bernadette Bascom in collaboration with innovators at Microsoft, this app based program blends music, breath control, rhythm, and physicality to make language learning joyful, memorable, and effective. Available in the Apple Store, Speak Music as Language uses music as a universal tool to help children find their voices, literally and figuratively, while fostering deeper connection between home and school environments.

With your support, we aim to:

  • Reach an additional 10,000 children with disabilities by 2026
  • Equip 5,000 parents and educators with tools and training to support consistent, effective speech development
  • Expand to 10 school districts and early childhood programs in under resourced communities
  • Demonstrate measurable improvements in speech clarity, confidence, and engagement in early learners

This project is more than an app. It's a bridge between science, music, and love. A tool that gives children the power to communicate, express themselves, and be heard.
